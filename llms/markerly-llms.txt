# Markerly — Influencer Marketing Agency

> Markerly is a full-service influencer marketing agency running creator campaigns since 2012 — strategy, vetted creators, content, activation, and real-time measurement. Two specialties set Markerly apart: influencer campaigns for government agencies, public health, and nonprofits (advocacy), and bilingual/multicultural creator campaigns.

## Services
- [Campaign Management](https://markerly.com/campaign-management): End-to-end managed influencer campaigns — strategy, sourcing, vetting, contracts, content approvals, activation, reporting.
- [Advocacy & Public Sector](https://markerly.com/advocacy): Influencer campaigns for government agencies, public health, and nonprofits — creator vetting, brand safety, regional and bilingual targeting.
- [For Influencers](https://markerly.com/influencers): Join Markerly's creator network.
